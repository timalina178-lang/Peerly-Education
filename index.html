Why we started

Growing up, we all went to tutoring. Too often, it was boring and hard to focus.

We started Peerly Education to make lessons more enjoyable, personal and effective. Students learn more when they feel comfortable asking questions and want to be there.

Tim & Evan

Our philosophy

At Peerly, we believe students learn best when they enjoy learning.

We are high-achieving students ourselves, so we know what keeps a lesson interesting, what helps a concept click and what makes learning feel like less of a chore.

Academic progress matters, and so does whether students enjoy the process and parents feel confident in the support. We want students to build confidence, take a genuine interest in learning and improve at school along the way.
