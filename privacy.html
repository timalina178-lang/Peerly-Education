Privacy information

How we handle enquiries

Peerly Education uses Google Forms to receive tutoring enquiries. The form may ask for a parent or guardian’s name, email address, phone number, the child’s year level, tutoring subject, lesson preference and any optional information the parent chooses to provide.

Why we collect this information

We use enquiry details to respond to the parent or guardian, understand the tutoring support requested and arrange a trial lesson. Please do not include a child’s full name or sensitive personal information in the form.

Where information is stored

Responses are handled through Google Forms and stored in the Peerly Education Google account. Google’s own privacy terms also apply when the form is used.

Access, correction and deletion

Parents and guardians can ask to see, correct or delete the personal information they have submitted by contacting Peerly Education.

Complete before publication

Add the business contact email, the legal name of the person or entity responsible for the information, how long enquiry records are kept and any other required Australian privacy disclosures.
