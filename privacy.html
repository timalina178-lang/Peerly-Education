Privacy information

How we handle enquiries

Peerly Education receives tutoring enquiries by text message, phone and email. A parent or guardian may choose to share their name, contact details, the child’s year level, tutoring subject, lesson preference and other information that helps us understand the support they are looking for.

Why we collect this information

We use enquiry details to respond to the parent or guardian, understand the tutoring support requested and arrange a trial lesson. Please do not send a child’s full name or sensitive personal information.

Where information is stored

Messages and emails are stored in the accounts and devices Peerly Education uses to respond to enquiries. Enquiry records are kept for up to six months, then deleted.

Access, correction and deletion

Parents and guardians can ask to see, correct or delete the personal information they have submitted by contacting Peerly Education.

Who is responsible

Tim is responsible for enquiry information. To ask a privacy question or request access, correction or deletion, email peerlyeducation@gmail.com or call 0403 783 818.
